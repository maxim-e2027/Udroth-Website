Kwoden, are the people that came second into the world during the [[Age of Creation]]. They came together with the [[Aradh]] during the [[Demonic Invasion]], the reason varies between faiths: uranists and dhinists claim that they were seduced by promises of power, while the [[Aradh Cults|aradh worshippers]] belive that the kwoden were trapped in the material world by [[The Stars]].

After the loss of the Aradh during the [[Demonic Invasion]], the [[First Aethliri Empire]] banished kwoden into [[Arwadek]] and into the [[Kwod]] - "exile" in kwoden language. These are the most inhospitable lands in Udroth and living there was meant as a punishment for joining the Aradh. Some kwoden, who changed sides during the war joined [[The Heroes]] settled in [[Izbred]] and wee forgiven by the Empire for their transgression. 

In the coming time, some of the kwoden would be allowed to settle in Dhinaem, while during the early [[Age of Chaos]] kwoden refugees were accepted by the [[Urmyzhar Empire]] in western [[Urandayed]]

Faiths among the kwoden are different. Some still worship their ancient gods, the [[Aradh]]. These [[Aradh Cults|cults]] are most prominent in [[Arwadek]], where the hold of the [[Aethliri Empire|Empire]] was weakest. Some were converted into the imperial faith of [[Dhinism]], especially in the Lower [[Kwod]]. The kwoden of Izbred worship [[The Heroes]] like their [[Yanadr|yanadr]] neighbors. On the borders of kwoden lands, numerous [[Syncretic Faiths|syncretic]] cults have appeared. 

Kwoden are of middle height, their appearance varies greatly on the region of [[Kwod]] and [[Arwadek]], as kwoden are the race that most often intermarried with others to escape the laws that restrained them to the shaded lands. For example, kwoden in the [[Kwod]] are taller and even paler than their arwadeki relatives due to aethlir blood.
