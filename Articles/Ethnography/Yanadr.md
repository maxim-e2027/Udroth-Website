The Yanadr were the first people to settle Udroth during its creation. In the Dawn Age they participated in building the world together with their [[Old Gods]]. 

They are mainly found in [[Urandayed]] and [[Izbred]], although communities in [[Dhinaem]] also exist. 

Most of them worship the [[Old Gods]] in one form or another, which often put them at odds with the [[Aethliri Empire]].

Three main branches of Yanadr exist: Uranic Yanadr, Izbredic Yanadr, and the Dirrha.

- **Uranic Yanadr**. The most numerous of the Yandr, the inhabitants of [[Urandayed]] and the most staunch supporters of Uran and the [[Old Gods]]. They often rebelled against the [[Aethliri Empire]].
- **Izbredic Yanadr.** A group of Yanadr that left Urandayed during the [[Age of Creation]] and settled [[Izbred]] has mixed views of the [[Old Gods]], ranging from reverence close to the practices of their Urandayedi relatives to indifference due to the [[Old Gods]]' departure from Udroth. Their main pantheon consists of [[The Heroes|Heroes]] – mortals ascended to godhood
- **The Dirrha**. The smallest of the three groups, the Dirrha never worshipped Uran or the [[Old Gods]], settling in remote reaches of [[Dhinaem]] where eternal rain pours from the World's Ceiling.

In appearance, Yanadr are usually shorter and stouter than aethlir, and have more body and facial hair. The prevalent hair colors are red, brown and black, their skin is brownish, but can be darker in some regions of [[Urandayed]], and almost white in [[Izbred]]
