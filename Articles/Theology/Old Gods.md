The Old Gods, (a.k.a. Uran's Court, the Architects, the Rebellious Builders, Wyrms' Locks) are the set of deities worshipped mostly by the [[Yanadr]], that have actively participated in creation of Udroth together with the [[Yanadr]] ancestors. 

[[Uranism|Uranists]] view them as benevolent benefactors and ancient leaders of their people, and hope for their return at the end of times. 

[[Dhinism|Star Worshippers]] think that they created the world only by the [[The Stars|Stars]]' orders and using their designs. They were exiled from Udroth after joining [[War of the Wyrms|Uran's Rebellion]].

Views on the Old Gods among [[Izbredic Hero Cult]]s is divided: worshippers of Lerauna and Dunger still honor them, while followers of Vyhkirg, Aalyran and Yazryala believe them to be unworthy of worship due to their failure to protect Izbred from the [[Demonic Invasion]].

[[Aradh Cults]] members view the entire existence of Udroth as evil, as in their worldview it is a prison to make divine spirits suffer and be mortal. Therefore they view the Old Gods as locks placed by wyrms Uran and Dhin upon the cage that is material reality. Breaking these locks is the central endeavour of all Aradh-worshippers thought.

After the defeat in the [[War of the Wyrms]] the Old Gods fled the material world.

The Old Gods are the following:

**Uran**, the Flamebringer, the Fire of Liberty, the Allfather, the Red

Uran is a wyrm, and his element is fire. He also is the chief and the eldest of the Old Gods. Creation of Udroth was his idea and he led the [[Yanadr]] in the [[Age of Creation]]. His wife is Zunarydh, and with her he has a son, Zaymarub, and a daughter, Yrdhuna.

After [[War of the Wyrms|the battle with Dhin]] he lies asleep on the eastern edge of the [[Urandayed|Boiling Sea]] and when he wakes, the end times will begin.

**Zunarydh**, the Allmother, the Crawling Forest

Zunarydh is depicted in various sources as a tree, a wyrm, and a hybrid of the two. She is the goddess of life and plants. She gave birth to first [[Pyroflora]], [[Photoflora]], and by some accounts, [[Caligoflora]]. Her husband is Uran, and her children are Zaymarub and Yrdhuna. 

Some accounts claim that for some time she fought alongside Dhin in the [[War of the Wyrms]], until Mzurabil returned her to Uran's Host. 

**Myrybhur**, the Deep One, the One-Who-Remained, the Redeemed,

Myrybhur is the god of earth and minerals, and is depicted as a living mountain of crystals. He placed the minerals in the earth, and taught [[Yanadr]] to use bronze. He was the most ferocious foe of [[The Stars]] and refused to flee the world, hiding in the caves below [[Urandayed]] . 

His wife is Urmyzh, and his son is Viryar. 

**Zaymarub,** the Eternal Rebel, the First Dragon, the Flying Tide

Zaymarub is the only son of Uran and Zunarydh, and is a Dragon. His domains are the sea, war, and change. He often disagreed with his father and attempted to fix parts of Udroth that he viewed as mistakes. 

His Sister-Wife is Yrdhuna, with whom he had a son, Mzurabil, and a daughter, Urmyzh. All the dragons are also progeny of Zaymarub and Yrdhuna. 

**Yrdhuna**, the Great River, the Calmer and Worrier of Hearts

Yrdhuna is the daughter of Uran and Zunarydh, and is depicted either as a a proteus. She is the goddess of rivers, and the [[Great River]] in particular, as well as love. She has a son and a daughter: Mzurabil and Urmyzh.


**Mzurabil**, the Wise One, the All-Seeing, the One to Come. 

Mzurabil is the son of Zaymarub and Yrdhuna. He is a five-legged octopus, and his domain is wisdom. He often served as the arbiter in conflicts between Uran and Zaymarub. 

Together with Mzurabil he created  Arazymar out of pure flame.


**Arazymar**, the Living Flame, the Unborn, the Burning Bronze  

Arazymar is the son of Mzurabil, not born but created by his father with aid of Mzurabil. His domain is civilization, and it was he who taught the [[Yanadr]] to build homes, grow crops and make instruments. His visage is that of a living flame, sometimes depicted ina  bronze bowl to signify his conection to Mzurabil

**Urmyzh**, the Lady of Reeds and Dreams, The Hiding One, the Arcane Mist

Urmyzh is the daughter of Zaymarub and Yrdhuna and wife of Mzurabil. She takes the form of living mist and is the patron of dreams, marshes, and magic.
