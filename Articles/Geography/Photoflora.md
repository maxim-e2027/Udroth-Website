Photoflora is the name for plants that obtain energy from light of [[The Stars|the Sun and Stars]]. They are most prevalent in [[Izbred]] and [[Dhinaem]], but also exist in Upper and Lower [[Kwod]]. 

They are believed to be children of [[Old Gods|Zunarydh]] and [[The Stars|Dhin]].
