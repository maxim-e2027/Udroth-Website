Photoflora is the name for plants that obtain energy from light of [the Sun and Stars](The Stars). They are most prevalent in [[Izbred]] and [[Dhinaem]], but also exist in Upper and Lower [[Kwod]]. 

They are believed to be children of [Zunarydh](<Old Gods>) and [Dhin](<The Stars>).
