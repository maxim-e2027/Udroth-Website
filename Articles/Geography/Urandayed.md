Urandayed lies in the southeast of Udroth, and is the deepest region, aside from the Abyss below [[Arwadek]]. From there the [[Old Gods]] ruled the world in the [[Age of Creation]], and there their memory is the strongest. The region frequently rebelled against the [[Aethliri Empire]]s. 

The climate is hot due to abundance of volcanoes and geysers, and wet due to proximity to the Boiling Sea and the [[Great River]]. Rains are often, and the seasons are not pronounced. Large areas are covered by forests of [[Pyroflora]] and the region serves as the breadbasket of the lower flow of the [[Great River]]. 


Urandayed lies on the coastline of the Boiling Sea, with Nurymad and Ynar seas protruding from east to west into the continent. It is split into Western Urandayed and Eastern Urandayed by Myrburaz mountains

Urandyaed has one large landmass, and several surrounding as well as island chains.
-  **Uymil**. A small landmass in the southwest of the Ynar Sea
- **Vyranar**. A landmass on the border of the Ynar and the Boiling seas
- **Zauyrem**. A landmass between the Nurymad and Boiling seas
- **Ubdaned Archipelago**. A chain of large islands stretching from Ebudray to Vyranar. 

Most of population is [[Yanadr]], but during early [[Age of Chaos]] [[Kwoden]] refugees came from the north, as well as [[Kilruz]] invaders settling Myrburaz mountains.
