The Great River flows from Rakzin Sea in [[Kilruzurtar]] to the Boiling Sea in [[Urandayed]]. 

It serves as the largest trade artery for all Udroth, as it is connected to almost all significant bodies of water. 

It has only few waterfalls, which are located in [[Arwadek]] and feed the Rwekad Sea. 

Upon reaching the Boiling Sea the river splits into myriad branches, forming a delta known as the "Hundred Streams".
