Caligoflora is the name for plants and fungi that feed on nutrients already made by other plants, that are dissolved in flowing water. It grows in the dark and is the sole source of food for people of [[Kwod]], [[Arwadek]] and [[Kilruzurtar]].

It is believed to be created by [Zunarydh](<Old Gods>) out of pity for those living in the dark, or by the [[Aradh]] corrupting [[Pyroflora]].
