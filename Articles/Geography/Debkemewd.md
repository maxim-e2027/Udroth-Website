Debkemewd is a region is southwestern [[Arwadek]]. It lies to the north of Kawadla riverlands and is mostly a desert, town and cities existing only along the river. To the north it is separated by mountains from the Rwekad Sea. To the northweast lie the marshes of Wemk.
