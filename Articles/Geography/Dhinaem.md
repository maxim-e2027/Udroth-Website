Dhinaem lies in the north-east of Wyrmsland. Throughout history it was the heart of the Aethliri Empire, and one of the most developed regions in the known world. This was thanks to abundant light channeled by crystals in the world's ceiling. 

The climate is mostly temperate, with rain and rarely snow, making it good for agriculture. Forests of light-dependent flora and fertile grasslands are commonplace there. 

The population is mostly Aethlir, although some Yanadr and Kwoden can be found. 

There are 4 big landmasses in Dhinaem: Trödaem, Ödhreth, Maedhönaem, Möthdhön.
