Dhinaem lies in the north-east of Udroth. Throughout history it was the heart of the [[Aethliri Empire]], and one of the most developed regions in the known world. This was thanks to abundant light channeled by crystals in the World's Ceiling. 

The climate is mostly temperate, with rain and rarely snow, making it good for agriculture. Forests of [[Photoflora]] and fertile grasslands are commonplace there. 

There are 4 big landmasses in Dhinaem: Trödaem, Ödhreth, Maedhönaem, Möthdhön.
