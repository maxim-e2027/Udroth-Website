Arwadek lies in the middle of Udroth. It was the place, from where the [[Aradh]] came into the world during the [[Demonic Invasion]], and where the surviving demons hid after their defeat. It is the crossroads of the world, connected to [[Kwod]], [[Izbred]] and [[Urandayed]].

Arwadek is a giant rift with the Rwekad Sea at the center. Through deepest reaches it is connected to the unexplored and uninhabitted Abyss, which goes unimaginaby deep below. 

Light is very scarce in Arwadek, but countless rivers and streams collect nutrients enough for [[Caligoflora]] to flourish. The land is very mountainous, and rivers converge in the Rwekad Sea. 

Arwadek is almost exclusively inhabitted by [[Kwoden]], as none others want to live in that shadowed land.
