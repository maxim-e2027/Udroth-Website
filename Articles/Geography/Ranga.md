Ranga lies in the western reach of the Herowood in northern [[Izbred]], along the river Vert. It is populated by [kwoden](Kwoden) who mostly worship [Dunger as their main god, but also worship all other heroes. ](<Izbredic Hero Cult>)

It lives on lumber, trade, and raiding by those who gave the Oath of Dunger. In the [[Age of Chaos]] overthrew its king to institute a tribal republic lead by lawspeakers.
