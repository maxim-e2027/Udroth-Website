War of the Wyrms is the second cyclopean conflict during the [[Age of Creation]], following the [[Demonic Invasion]]. [[Yanadr]] and the [[Old Gods]] viewed Udroth as their creation, and wished to continue the building that was interrupted by the attack. But [[Aethlir]] and their god [Dhin](Dhinism) wished to make a fortress out of Udroth, to continue the fight against the [[Aradh]] even outside the material world. For that they wished to institute an [empire](<Aethliri Empire>) with [Dhin](Dhinism) at the head. 

A war broke out, and [[The Heroes]]of [[Izbred]] were split: those who were still spiteful of the [[Old Gods]] joined the [[Aethlir]], while those still revering them joined the [[Yanadr]]. 

Neither side could triumph, until the Battle of the Wyrms. In [[Kilruzurtar]] [Dhin](Dhinism) and [Uran](Uranism) met and fought, until Dhin was dead, and Uran was deeply wounded, after which he either died of his wounds, or had to retreat beyond the [Boiling Sea](Urandayed) to recover. 

The [yanadr](Yanadr) were without their leader, and different [[Old Gods]] claimed seniority. The army was disorganized, and the [aethlir](Aethlir) used that to their advantage. The followers of Dhin quickly found a new leader – an emperor, who is appointed directly by the light of Sun and [[The Stars]], and thus holds legitimacy of Dhin. 

The [[Old Gods]] had lost, and were forced to escape outside of the world, except Myrybhur, who hid in caverns below the mountains of [Myrybhurazh](Urandayed). The [[First Aethliri Empire]] was proclaimed, and the [[Age of Creation]] ended.
