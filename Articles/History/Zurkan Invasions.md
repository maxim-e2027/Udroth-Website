Zurkan Invasions is a series of conflicts in early [[Age of Chaos]] that were caused by migrations of [[Kilruz]] under command of [[Kilruz Prophets|Zurkan and his sons]]. 

The migrations were caused by religious conflict between the followers of [[Kilruz Prophets|Zurkan]] and the [[Kilruz Faiths|Temple of Zin]]. A great war was fought betwen the sides, which ended in a stalemate. Zurkanites were allowed to retain some lands in [[Kilruzurtar]], but most of them were to leave. 

The first invasion was that of [[Dhinaem]]. Kilruz crossed the badlands of Titzintar and marched into Aeatör Vales in Trödaem. They conquered a city, but Zurkan was killed in an ambush. The [[Kilruz]] entered a frenzy and killed most of the city's inhabitants. The [[Third Aethliri Empire]] sent an army to stop the invaders, but the [[Kilruz]] triumphed at Aeatör Pass, which caused [[The Great Sealing]] and collapse of the [[Aethliri Empire]]. 

After that Zurkan's three sons split. The eldest, Ottr remained in Trödaem to finish its conquest. The other brothers, Kruzikil and Zokaer decided to explore the lands down the [[Great River]] there they invaded Upper [[Kwod]]. There, the brothers' opinion on how to proceed split. The young brother, Zokaer, wished to make peace with local [[Kwoden]], and declared himself Lord-Protector of the Commonwealth of Upper Kwod. Kruzikil was enraged and viewed his brother as a traitor. He took men loyal to him and pressed on along the [[Great River]]. The journey was devastated, as the Kilruz were beset by imperial legions, [[Exarchate of Koldwealm]], [[Kwoden]] in [[Arwadek]]. 

After being decimated, Kruzikil's [[Kilruz]] finally reached [[Urandayed]]. There they were attacked by the forces of the [[Urandayedic Confederation]]. During the march through the bogs of [[Urmyzhar Empire|Urmyzhar]] many tribes separated from Kruzikil, and would later be known as the Forsaken. 

Kruzikil reached Myryburaz, and was able to conquer the Sacred Vale of Myrybhur. The prophet died from his wounds, but from his corpse sprouted a mushroom , that gave life to many later generations of [[Kilruz]].
