The Great Sealing is the watershed event that separates the [[Age of History]] from the [[Age of Chaos]].  It happened in year 916 of the Age of History(916 AoH), when the emperor of the [[Third Aethliri Empire]] sealed away the capital city [[Aethlirin]] fearing for its safety in the face of [[Zurkan Invasions]].

By the 10th century of the [[Age of History]] the [[Aethliri Empire|Empire]] was already troubled. A civil war was fought in [[Dhinaem]], depleting imperial resources, remote governors squabbled amongst themselves for land and control of trade. 

When [[Kilruz]] under the leadership of their prophet-chief [[Kilruz Prophets|Zurkan]] invaded Trödaem from northeast, the emperor assembled a host of his legionnaires and armies of loyal governors under the leadership of his only son, Öraethin, and two imperial generals: Ghaerösh and Dhaerniko, the governor of Möthnaerin. The Izbredic Guard of [[Aethlirin]] was also sent with them.

The imperial army faced the zurkanites in the Aeatör Pass. Despite being more numerous and having a very defendable position the imperial army was defeated, not having expected the foe to scale cliffs so easily. [[Kilruz]] climbers ambushed the archers under command of the prince and killed all of them, including the only heir to the throne. 

At the time, the Emperor Adharöth II  had a vision from [[Dhinism|Dhin]] stating that the invaders will triumph and the only chance to preserve the Empire is to isolate the capital by breaking the pillar connecting the World's Ceiling to the ground. Only the imperial palace, temples and gardens were located in the World's Ceiling and could remain undiscovered. 

The empire had lost all its governing bodies including the high priesthood except the Senate, which was located in Möthnaerin. But what was worse, due to [[Aethlirin]]'s religious significance, a new emperor couldn't be crowned anywhere but there. This led to the inability of a new dynasty to be founded and the following breaking of the [[Third Aethliri Empire]]
