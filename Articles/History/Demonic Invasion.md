At some point in the [[Age of Creation]], for one reason or another [[Aradh]] together with [[Kwoden]] came into Udroth in [[Arwadek]] and declared themselves sovereigns of the land. They attacked the [[Old Gods]] and [[Yanadr]] in [[Urandayed]] and [[Izbred]], enslaving those they could. This event would become the catalyst for many transformations that would make Udroth what it is today. 

Uran led the [[Yanadr]] resistance to the demons' invasion. The [[Old Gods]] were able to hold the attackers on the borders of [[Urandayed]], but the [[Yanadr|yanadr]] in [[Izbred]] were left on their own and mostly enslaved by the conquerors. This led to many people of Izbred to forsake the [[Old Gods]].

The war turned to a stalemate, with [[Kwoden]] slowly wearing out [[Yanadr|yanadr]] defenses, and pushing them deeper into [[Urandayed]]. Gods clashed with demons, but none could emerge victorious.

At that time [[Dhinism|Dhin Sunwyrm]] convinced [[The Stars]] to intervene and arrived in [[Dhinaem]] with a host of [[Aethlir]]. They started striking on the [[Kwoden]] from the north, forcing the demons to slow their advance in [[Urandayed]]. But this still was not enough to fully defeat the invaders.

That's when in [[Izbred]], several distinguished individuals, who would later be known as [[The Heroes]] set out to Urandayed to seek the [[Old Gods]]' help in liberating their enslaved peoples. On their way they convinced several [[Kwoden]] tribes to turn away from the [[Aradh]] and join the fight against them. 

[[The Heroes]] reached [[Urandayed]], and there the [[Old Gods]] bestowed them with divine power. With their newfound strength they led a rebellion in [[Izbred]] that put the invaders under assault from three sides, and eventually most of the demons were killed, while others hid in the abysses below [[Arwadek]].

But expulsion of the demons did not yet bring peace into Udroth, for [[The Stars]] and the [[Old Gods]] were united by mutual hatred of the [[Aradh]], who now were gone. This tension would lead into the [[War of the Wyrms]].
