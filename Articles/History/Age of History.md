Age of History is the second age in history of Udroth, that followed the [[Age of Creation]]. Written sources begin to appear at this time, although in the earlier times they are still very scarce. The Age of History can be divided into three major periods by the number of the [[Aethliri Empire]], and two wars between them.

- [[First Aethliri Empire]]. Times of imperial rule were somewhat uneventful. [Izbredic Heroes](<The Heroes>) lead crusades against remaining [aradh worshippers](<Aradh Cults>) in [[Arwadek]] and [[Kwod]]. Imperial inquisitors attempt to convert heretics in [[Urandayed]], [[Kwod]] and [[Arwadek]]. Their attempts are often thwarted by minor local rebellions. 
- **The Dragon War**. Dragons invade from [[Kilruzurtar]], initially praising [Uran](Uranism), but then switching to [Aradh Worship](Aradh Cults) upon reaching [[Arwadek]] due to its corrupting influence. [[Urandayed]] successfully rebells, but rejoins the [empire](Aethliri Empire) to defend against the dragons, in return demands religious tolerance
- [[Second Aethliri Empire]]. [Exarchs](Exarchate) rebuild the empire, but with significant regional autonomy. 
- **The Exarch Wars**. Autonomy leads to many conflicts erupting between the exarchates. Their rulers refuse to obey the emperor.
- [[Third Aethliri Empire]] Exarch of [[Izbred]] joins forces with the emperor in [[Aethlirin]] and reunites the empire. Now governors are much weaker, have less territory, and obey the emperor directly. Ended with [[The Great Sealing]] during [[Zurkan Invasions]]
