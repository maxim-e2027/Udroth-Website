Age of Chaos is the third and ongoing age in history of Udroth, that followed the [[Age of History]]. It began with [[The Great Sealing]], when the [aethliri emperor ](Aethliri Empire)sealed off the [capital](Aethlirin) to save it from [Zurkanites](Zurkan Invasions) which led to the collapse of the empire. 

The first years of the age were characterized by widespread anarchy, rapid birth and destruction of states, migrations and consequent wars. 

After the end of [[Zurkan Invasions]] the situation somewhat stabilized, but the empire was completely shattered, with almost no hope for reunification.
