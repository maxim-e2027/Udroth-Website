Urmyzhar Empire is a splinter state of the [[Urandayedic Confederation]] in the [[Age of Chaos]]. It seceded to better protect itself against the [[Zurkan Invasions]] and the [[Kilruz]] that settled in Myryburaz. 

It was located in western [[Urandayed]] and composed of two main regions: Urmyzhar marshes to the north of the [[Great River]] and the Lyrumyn jungles to the south. The Empire attempted to take a more centralized model than the Confederation as the external threat of the [[Kilruz]] invaders was very strong. An emperor was elected by a council of rulers, first of them being the Horned King of Lyrumyn, the First Speaker of Urmyzhar, and the King of New Wyrkw. New Wyrkw was a riverside kingdom founded by refugee [[Kwoden]]  [[Dhinism|sun-worshippers]] escaping the Dodecarchy in [[Arwadek]]. They were begrudgingly accepted because of the need for allies and lack of resources to fight a war on two fronts. 

The disagreements between the member peoples caused the empire to be very unstable, with frequent interregnums and infighting. Despite that, it was effective at combatting [[Kilruz]], and therefore existed for a long time.
